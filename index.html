      FAQ

      Common Questions

      Do I need a credit card for the free trial? +
      No card needed. 2-week free trial starts immediately. You only pay when you choose to upgrade.
      What payment methods are accepted? +
      Visa, Mastercard, Verve, bank transfer, USSD, mobile money — all via Paystack. Global cards accepted.
      How do podcast uploads to YouTube/TikTok/Facebook work? +
      After recording or editing your podcast, click the platform button (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook). MEERA authenticates with the platform API and uploads with your title, thumbnail and description automatically. Requires Pro plan.
      Are quizzes and trivia free? +
      Yes — all quizzes, personality tests, IQ tests and trivia are free forever for all users.
      How does the jobs board work? +
      Jobs are pulled from live APIs (Remotive, Adzuna) plus curated local listings. Filter by remote, Nigeria, internship or tech. Apply directly. Set email alerts for new jobs in your category.
      How do vendors get paid? +
      Vendors earn 80% per sale. Withdraw anytime to your bank account via Paystack. Processed within 24–48 hours.
